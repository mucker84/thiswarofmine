How Long Does a Cotton Sock Take to Decompose?

1–5 years
natural fibre, but dyes and elastane slow it

A cotton sock takes 1 to 5 years to decompose. Pure cotton is a natural fibre that microbes can break down, but a sock is never pure cotton.

Why does it take this long?

The elastane in the cuff, synthetic reinforcement in the heel and toe, and the dyes all resist decomposition. In a wet environment the process slows further, and the stretchy parts can outlast the cotton by years.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a cotton sock take to decompose?
A cotton sock takes approximately 1–5 years to decompose in nature.
Do cotton socks biodegrade?
The cotton portion does, but the elastic and synthetic reinforcements do not fully break down and leave microplastics behind.
Can old socks be recycled?
Yes — textile recycling programs accept worn socks. It's far better than sending them to landfill or nature.
