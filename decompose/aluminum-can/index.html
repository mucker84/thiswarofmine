How Long Does an Aluminum Can Take to Decompose?

200 years
infinitely recyclable — a waste to litter

An aluminum can takes around 200 years to break down in nature. But that number tells only half the story — because aluminum is one material that never needs to be wasted.

Why does it take this long?

Aluminum can be recycled endlessly with no loss of quality, using a fraction of the energy of making it new. A littered can is therefore a double loss: 200 years of pollution, and a valuable resource thrown away. A recycled can is often back on a shelf within weeks.

Frequently asked questions

How long does an aluminum can take to decompose?
An aluminum can takes approximately 200 years to decompose in nature.
Is aluminum recyclable forever?
Yes. Unlike plastic, aluminum can be melted and reused indefinitely without degrading.
How much energy does recycling a can save?
Recycling aluminum uses about 95% less energy than producing it from raw ore.
