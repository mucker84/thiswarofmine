How Long Does an Apple Core Take to Decompose?

2 months
fast — but roadside litter still harms wildlife

An apple core decomposes in around 2 months. As biodegradable food waste, nature reclaims it easily — insects, microbes, and fungi all feed on it and return its nutrients to the soil.

Why does it take this long?

That doesn't make it harmless to toss on the roadside. A rotting core attracts birds and small mammals toward traffic, and in dry or cold conditions it can linger much longer than two months, looking like litter the whole time.

Frequently asked questions

How long does an apple core take to decompose?
An apple core takes approximately 2 months to decompose in nature.
Is it OK to throw an apple core in nature?
It biodegrades, but it's still best avoided near roads and trails — it draws wildlife into danger and isn't native to every ecosystem.
How fast does an apple core compost?
In an active compost heap, a matter of weeks. On cold bare ground it can take several months.
