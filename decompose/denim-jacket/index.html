How Long Does a Denim Jacket Take to Decompose?

10–12 months
cotton is quick — the rivets and dye aren't

A denim jacket takes about 10 to 12 months to decompose — surprisingly fast, because denim is mostly cotton.

Why does it take this long?

But the cotton is the only quick part. The synthetic thread, metal rivets and buttons, and indigo dyes remain in the soil long after the fabric is gone. Modern stretch denim also contains elastane, which leaves microplastics behind. Fast fashion means billions of garments are discarded yearly.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a denim jacket take to decompose?
A denim jacket takes approximately 10–12 months to decompose in nature.
Is denim biodegradable?
The cotton is, within about a year. Rivets, synthetic thread, and dyes persist much longer.
How can I dispose of old denim responsibly?
Donate, upcycle, or use textile recycling — denim is often turned into insulation and new fabric.
