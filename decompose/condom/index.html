How Long Does a Condom Take to Decompose?

30 years
latex, but additives slow it dramatically

A latex condom takes about 30 years to decompose. Natural rubber latex sounds biodegradable, but a finished condom is far from natural.

Why does it take this long?

It's treated with stabilisers, lubricants, and sometimes spermicides that dramatically slow decomposition. Many condoms also contain synthetic materials. They should never be flushed — they clog systems and end up in waterways.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a condom take to decompose?
A condom takes approximately 30 years to decompose in nature.
Are condoms biodegradable?
Natural latex condoms slowly break down, but additives extend this to decades. Polyurethane condoms don't biodegrade at all.
Can you flush a condom?
Never. Condoms don't break down in water and cause blockages and marine pollution. Always bin them.
