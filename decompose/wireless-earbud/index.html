How Long Does a Wireless Earbud Take to Decompose?

1,000+ years
e-waste that never breaks down

A wireless earbud takes 1,000 years or more to decompose — if it ever really does. It represents the e-waste problem of our generation.

Why does it take this long?

Each bud is a composite of plastics, metals, and a sealed lithium battery. No single part breaks down naturally, and the battery makes it hazardous waste. Designed to be nearly impossible to repair or recycle, hundreds of millions are discarded as they wear out.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a wireless earbud take to decompose?
A wireless earbud takes approximately 1,000+ years to decompose in nature.
Can wireless earbuds be recycled?
Only through specialised e-waste programs — never in household recycling, because of the lithium battery.
Why are earbuds bad for the environment?
They combine plastics, metals, and batteries into a tiny sealed unit that can't be separated or repaired.
